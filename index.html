⚠ Lawful-use gate — read before continuing

This tool is built for self-audit: checking identifiers that belong to you. The following are deliberately not part of this tool:

  • Searching a third party's phone number or email address
  • Returning breach or leaked records for anyone
  • Harvesting photographs of people
  • Any lookup that requires bypassing a login or a paywall

How this works. Every check runs in your browser using publicly accessible, unauthenticated endpoints only. No data is stored, logged, transmitted to the operator, or shared with third parties. There is no backend. Closing this tab erases everything.

Scope limits. Breach data is not returned by this tool — you can check your own status via HaveIBeenPwned with your own API key, or via their website. Phone-number lookup, third-party breach aggregation and photograph harvesting are intentionally not implemented.

No warranty. Results reflect publicly visible information at the moment of the scan and may produce false positives or negatives. A clean result is not proof of a clean footprint.
